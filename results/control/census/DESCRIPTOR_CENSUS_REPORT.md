## 2. Headline findings (each verified against stage outputs)

1. **Implementation gate passed.** The census SR reproduces the manuscript's cached
   507-line benchmark to max |Δ| = 1.0e-12, which proves the SR row is the same
   estimator as the manuscript's Extended-Data benchmark (not an approximation).

2. **Readout specificity is universal, not NetITH-specific.** Every expression-based
   descriptor collapses on the AUC readout (med ρ = 0.008–0.031) while retaining a
   strong IC50 association. This is a *shared property* of expression-derived
   descriptors in GDSC2, and it is now the strongest available explanation of the
   manuscript's ln(IC50)-vs-AUC contrast — stronger than the readout-specific
   mechanism the Discussion currently attributes to NetITH alone.

3. **NetITH is not the strongest associated descriptor, but it is the cleanest.**
   Scalar descriptors (CytoTRACE proxy 0.384, MAD 0.381, Shannon 0.357) exceed
   NetITH (0.232) in median ρ — consistent with the manuscript's own MAD-baseline
   disclosure (focused MAD 0.274 on the 239-gene set). NetITH's differentiators are:
   (a) **lowest purity confounding** (max |ρ| = 0.093 across 5 Aran-2015 estimates;
   MAD 0.50, CytoTRACE 0.62, DNE 0.47 are strongly purity-driven); (b) **perfect
   directional consistency** (286/286 positive vs 274–275/286 for scalars); (c) the
   only descriptor whose drug association survives its design-specific Test 1
   (amplitude-matched null, manuscript cached).

4. **Test 1 exposes the information source.** SR's association does not depend on
   the specific correlation topology (emp p ≈ 1.0 vs 50 rewired graphs): its signal
   is carried by the expression weights, not by the edge set. DNE (p=0.67) and
   TF-activity (p=0.33) likewise fail their nulls at the conventional threshold —
   the topology/regulon structure contributes incrementally at best. NetITH's
   amplitude-matched null (p=5.7e-4) shows its signal is NOT carried by the
   per-sample expression amplitude distribution — the complementary claim to SR's.
   *Caveat:* null designs differ across rows (rewired vs regulon-shuffle vs
   amplitude-matched), so across-row Test-1 comparisons are qualitative.

5. **Test 4 transfer does not favour NetITH on GSE25066.** Shannon OR 1.67
   (p=0.007) is the only significant pCR transfer among the panel; NetITH stays
   0.86 (p=0.29) as reported in the manuscript. SR 1.19 (p=0.19) is the closest
   network descriptor to significance.
